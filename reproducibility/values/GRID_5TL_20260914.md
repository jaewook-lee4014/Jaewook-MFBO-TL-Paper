# Fig. 3 (126-condition grid) re-run for the five retained TL classes — 2026-09-14

Fig. 3 (`paper_figures/fig3_grid_final_regret.pdf`) previously took its TL family from
`Progressive / KnowledgeDistillation / PseudoLabeling`, all three of which are dropped by the
five-surrogate decision. The whole 126-cell grid was therefore re-run for the new TL set:

| display name | code class |
|---|---|
| Frozen-representation transfer | `DNGOJoint` |
| Pretrain-then-Joint | `TwoStageJoint` |
| End-to-End Joint | `DNGOGradient` |
| Soft Parameter Sharing | `SoftParameterSharing` |
| Domain Adaptation (MMD) | `DomainAdaptationMMD` |

Nothing in the existing `results_grid/` tree was touched: the new runs went into the sibling
tree `results_grid_5tl/`. The GP rows (MFGP, SparseMFGP, DKL) are taken verbatim from the
existing `figrepo/results/grid/cells/` files, so the `MFGP variants vs MFGP baseline` map
(panel c) is bit-identical to the previous figure.

## Protocol (identical to the original grid)

* Arm `blr_replace__hf_argmin` (`HF_HEAD=blr_replace LF_TARGET=hf_argmin WARM=0 ACT=tanh
  WARM_EPOCHS=50`), CPU, 1 thread per run.
* Seeds `42 43 44 45 46` and `47 48 49 50 51` (10 seeds per cell, two five-seed task lines).
* Budget 50, `init_budget` 10, cost ratio rho = 0.1, per-fidelity candidate masking
  (`pools.grid_cell`), 126 cells `cell_00 … cell_125` from
  `figrepo/results/grid/grid_manifest.csv` (R^2 x top-10 agreement).
* 126 cells x 5 classes x 10 seeds = **6,300 runs** (1,260 task lines).

### Sentences in the manuscript that change

`main.tex` Results 2.3 (L293-L300 area) and Discussion (L457 area):

* "positive in $81$ of $126$ cells and the remaining $45$ cells tie" — **still exactly true**.
* "mean advantage $0.064$" -> **$0.072$**.
* "against the MFGP variants the advantage narrows to $60$ cells, with $65$ ties and one
  reversal (mean $0.017$)" -> **$66$ cells, $60$ ties and no reversal (mean $0.025$)**.
* "Spearman rank correlation $\rho = -0.79$ ... $R^2$ ($\rho = -0.15$)" -> **$-0.81$ ... $-0.09$**.
* "falling from about $0.20$ at zero agreement to zero at full agreement while its profile
  against $R^2$ stays between $0.03$ and $0.09$" -> **from about $0.22$ ... stays between
  $0.04$ and $0.09$**.
* "against the MFGP variants the two dependences are $\rho = -0.62$ and $+0.04$" ->
  **$\rho = -0.78$ and $+0.11$**.
* "the variants themselves improve on the baseline in $81$ cells" — **unchanged**.
* "is never worse and wins in two thirds of the conditions" (Discussion) — **unchanged**
  (81/126 = 64.3 %); note this holds for the *family* (best of the five). It is **not** true of
  `DNGOJoint` on its own, which loses one cell (cell_104: R^2 0.30, agreement 0.4; advantage -0.0022) and wins 76.
* The caption's "$126$ conditions of ten seeds each" and the per-panel "TL better in 81/126
  | ties 45" annotations are regenerated from the data and remain correct.
